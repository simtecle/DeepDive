# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-directed learners who want to understand a new topic without sorting through noisy or disconnected YouTube results. They arrive with a concrete subject in mind and need a trustworthy sequence that makes the next step obvious.

## Product Purpose

DeepDive turns a topic into a maintained YouTube curriculum across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels. Success means users can begin quickly, understand the intended order, and request useful coverage when a topic is missing.

## Brand Personality

Curious, precise, composed. DeepDive should encourage discovery without becoming playful, gamified, or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue SaaS dashboards and decorative AI gradients
- Gamified learning interfaces with points, streaks, or achievement chrome
- Glassmorphism, excessive card grids, and ornamental motion
- Interfaces that make the learning path feel less important than the application shell

## Design Principles

- Make the next learning step unmistakable.
- Let real video content carry the visual richness.
- Keep discovery inviting while preserving focus.
- Explain system states where the user acts.
- Prefer familiar, accessible controls over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, minimum 44px touch targets, reduced motion, sufficient text and placeholder contrast, and layouts from 320px upward.
